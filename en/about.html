About

A free, open-source, privacy-first online tool

Why we built this

Compressing images is a daily need: getting under email attachment limits, meeting upload caps on application portals, speeding up web pages, or freeing up phone storage. But nearly every online compressor uploads your images to its servers first — which is a privacy risk for photos of people, IDs, contracts or internal documents.

So we built this: all compression happens inside your browser. Your images never leave your device. No sign-up, no fees, no ZIP archives — one image in, one image out.

How it works

  1. Decode — the browser natively decodes your file (JPG / PNG / WebP / BMP).
  2. Redraw and re-encode — the image is drawn to a Canvas and re-encoded as WebP / JPEG / PNG at your chosen quality. WebP is 25–35% smaller than JPEG at equal quality, so it is the default.
  3. Target size mode — binary-searches the quality parameter (~8 iterations) to find the best quality that still fits under your KB limit.

No server-side computation is involved, so it even works offline.

Privacy bonus

Redrawing on Canvas discards the original EXIF metadata (capture time, device, GPS location). That means compressing also strips location privacy data from your photos.
