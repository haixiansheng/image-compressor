Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Our core commitment

Your images never leave your device. All image processing (decoding, resizing, re-encoding) is performed locally inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or transmitted to any server.

2. What we collect

This tool itself collects no personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not collect the images you process
  • We do not collect names, email addresses or phone numbers
  • No registration or login is required
  • We set no tracking cookies of our own (third-party ads may set their own — see below)

3. Third-party advertising

This site may display third-party ads (such as Google AdSense). These providers may:

  • Use cookies or similar technologies to serve personalised ads based on your browsing history
  • Collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type and visit time

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. You can manage personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out via aboutads.info.

4. Cookies

This site itself sets no cookies. Any cookie notices come from third-party ad providers. Disabling cookies in your browser does not affect the core functionality of this tool.

5. Logs and analytics

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may keep access logs (IP address, time of access). See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We collect no additional logs ourselves.

6. About EXIF data

Compression discards the original EXIF metadata (including GPS coordinates) as a technical consequence of Canvas redrawing. This works in your favour: it protects your location privacy.

7. Children's privacy

Intended for general audiences and not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; any update will be reflected by a new effective date on this page. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/haixiansheng/image-compressor/issues.
